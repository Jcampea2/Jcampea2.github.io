Film Blog #1: Knvies Out: Wake Up Dead Man

I was able right before thanksgiving to go to the see the new knives out movie. Which is currently in theaters but will shortly be out on netflix for all viewing pleasure. Much like the first two movies Knives out: Wake up dead men contiunes Rain Johnson who-dunnit series. The basic plot being that a priest from a small town is killed in a private storage closet which only has one door. Making this a locked room mystery. Without spoiling anything the film has a way of playing with the audience in the same way as the other knvies out films. It once again left me completely complied by the story it told along with the feeling that the case didn't feel like it had any loopholes or contrivances in it
(like other mystery movies). I will more than likely give a full review a later date. Once the film releases
but for now all I can say is I very much enjoyed Knvies Out: Wake Up Dead Man
